How the bot trades

Every minute a background job on the server checks each running bot. It loads completed candles for your markets, runs the same EMA, RSI, MACD, ADX and structure checks as the dashboard, and looks for a BUY or SELL setup whose score is at or above your minimum.

A signal opens one simulated position per market (never a duplicate for the same candle). Size comes from your risk per trade and the stop distance (ATR × your multiplier); the target is the stop distance × your reward:risk. Stops and targets are checked against candle highs and lows, and if one candle touches both the stop is assumed to hit first.

If the daily loss limit or max drawdown is reached, every simulated position is closed and the bot halts. The emergency stop does the same on demand. Nothing on this page, or in the server code behind it, connects to a broker.

The confirmation engine

A setup needs every condition to agree. Otherwise the answer is NO TRADE.

BUY setup

Price above EMA 200, EMA 20 above EMA 50, RSI above 50, MACD bullish, ADX above 20, bullish structure (higher highs and higher lows).

SELL setup

Price below EMA 200, EMA 20 below EMA 50, RSI below 50, MACD bearish, ADX above 20, bearish structure (lower highs and lower lows).

Score out of 100

EMA alignment 20, RSI 15, MACD 15, ADX 10, structure 20, multi-timeframe 20. 90+ very strong, 75–89 strong, 60–74 moderate, below 60 no trade.

Not a win probability

The score measures how many technical checks agree. It does not predict the outcome of a trade, and past setups do not guarantee future results.

FAQ

Do you guarantee accuracy or profit?

No. Signals are analytical information, not financial advice. Trading carries a risk of loss.

Do signals repaint?

No. Signals use completed candles only, so a past signal never changes.

Does TradeMate place trades for me?

Never with real money. TradeMate has no broker connection and cannot place a real order. The optional Auto Bot trades a simulated paper account with virtual money, so you can test a strategy without risk.

Where does the price data come from?

Twelve Data, fetched by a secure server so the API key is never exposed. Each panel shows whether the data is live, delayed, stale or unavailable, with the time of the latest candle. Some intervals or instruments may not be available on every Twelve Data plan.

Are the performance stats real?

Backtested and live results are reported separately with sample sizes. Nothing is shown until it has been measured.

TradeMate AI provides technical analysis for education and information only. It is not financial advice. Confirmation scores are not win probabilities. Markets can move against any setup.
